# LoloBuy official FAQ research — 2026-10-20

Scope: lolobuysheet.nl. Platform facts come from LoloBuy's own public help content, retrieved afresh, not competitor claims. All 20 public `helpMenuDetail?id=…` responses returned `code: 200`. Browser-rendered help pages have the equivalent public `/helpCenter/{id}` address. Search retrieval of the client-rendered help pages produced empty or unavailable content, so the official public JSON was used and archived.

Deliverable: `lolobuy-official.json` contains 20 named sources, 24 distinct English FAQ answers, per-answer source IDs, and editorial caveats. Raw official content is retained in `124*.json`; `official-help-text.txt` is a readable extraction.

## Useful verified details

- Buying progresses from seller-link or manual entry to item/domestic payment, purchasing, warehouse inspection, and a separate international parcel submission.
- Same-seller items checked out together can share domestic freight, with extra charges for unusually large/heavy purchases.
- Before seller dispatch, option changes can be negotiated. After dispatch, a return plus new order may be needed.
- `Pending for Confirmation` requires the buyer to answer an order message. `Received` precedes completed inspection; `In Stock` indicates stored inventory.
- Standard QC checks visible order attributes, with exclusions for sealed goods and professional electronics. The no-inspection option provides no QC photos.
- Package removal retains necessary protection; removal is a choice, not a promised freight saving.
- Freight is estimated before final settlement; packed weight, dimensions and route-specific volumetric rules matter.
- Eligible no-reason returns have a stated 120-hour window from the next hour after `Stocked in`, with seller approval, condition/packaging and possible freight/handling costs.
- Approved refunds generally go to the platform balance. A narrow PayPal exception does not establish general current payment availability.
- 1688 minimum quantities and inspection rules differ from individual retail purchases.

## Conflicts that must remain visible

1. Storage: dedicated storage/current status say 180 days, while the older buying guide still says 90. Refer to the actual item deadline rather than promising one unconditional period.
2. Photo quantity: standard inspection says 3–6; service overview says 5 plus 5 on request, with parcel photos described separately. Avoid a universal photo-count promise.
3. Parcel after-sales: the same page contains 7/45-day wording and a 3-day delivered warning. Recommend immediate reporting and written case-specific confirmation.
4. Weight example: source arithmetic/units are inconsistent. Do not reproduce it as a tariff or numerical worked example.
5. Service-page speed/price marketing is not independent proof. Do not claim best rates, assured delivery, verified seller quality or blanket free returns.

All customer-facing answers are original paraphrases with concrete actions. No made-up coupon, platform price, delivery promise or refund guarantee is included. The FAQ is about LoloBuy; product inventory remains cnfanssp.com and is outside this research task.
