## Ten distinct targets

1. How to use a LoloBuy spreadsheet — discovery and a usable shortlist.
2. How to order on LoloBuy — exact link, option checks and payment stages.
3. LoloBuy QC photos — order identity, measurements, visible inspection and limits.
4. LoloBuy shipping costs — real parcel inputs and route comparison; no fake live calculator.
5. LoloBuy warehouse — QC status, storage terms and consolidation.
6. LoloBuy sizing — seller measurements and category-specific fit checks.
7. LoloBuy tracking — distinguish domestic order and international parcel stages.
8. LoloBuy returns/refunds — stage, evidence, eligibility and current procedure.
9. LoloBuy fees/budget — full cost components and payment checks.
10. Is LoloBuy legit / seller review checks — assess evidence without an unsupported verdict.

The JSON file contains original detailed briefs, proposed titles/descriptions, search intents and official-source leads. The official research agent is re-verifying help pages before operational claims are written. Article briefs are not a substitute for that verification.

## Official verification and finished copy

The official research agent fetched 20 current public help documents successfully from the official helpMenuDetail endpoint on 2026-10-20. The ten finished English articles are in `articles-en.json` and use source IDs aligned with the project `sources.json`. Every operational claim was grounded in those help documents; general comparison/checklist advice is original editorial guidance. The articles preserve conflicts in official storage duration, photo allowances and parcel claim deadlines rather than inventing a single guarantee. Body plus checklist length is approximately 600–690 words per article. No competitor prose, fabricated testimonials or numerical search-volume claims were copied.
