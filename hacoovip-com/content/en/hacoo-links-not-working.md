## Identify which part of the link is failing
When Hacoo links are not working, describe the result before changing anything. Does the browser show an unavailable page? Does the link open an app but lose the product? Does a product appear with a different title or picture? Is the item visible but unavailable for your address? These outcomes need different checks. Repeatedly tapping the same link will not distinguish them, and replacing the URL with a homepage can hide the problem instead of fixing it.

Keep a copy of the original link and note where you found it. A creator post, an independent spreadsheet and an order record have different purposes. Hacoo VIP is an independent guide, and its catalogue buttons can lead to a separate product catalogue. Identify the domain before assuming that every page labelled Hacoo belongs to the official app. The service where you placed an order remains the place to check that order.
## Check the address without changing its identity
Copy the full address using the visible sharing or copying control available on the source page. A line break, truncated message or punctuation mark can make an otherwise valid link unusable. Compare the copied address with the original. Preserve the part that identifies the product and any required routing parameters. Do not remove unfamiliar parameters indiscriminately: some are tracking labels, while others may be essential to selecting the item or opening the intended app route.

Read the hostname carefully. A familiar logo or product name does not establish ownership of the destination. If the page asks for a password, payment details or an installation unrelated to the task, stop and verify the service through its established entry point. A broken product link does not justify bypassing browser security warnings. Avoid public link expanders when the address contains account-specific information or a private sharing token.
## Distinguish browser opening from app handoff
Some links are intended to hand a destination from a browser to an installed app. A failure can therefore happen after the browser has opened the correct address. Observe whether the app launches, whether it recognises the expected account and whether the item survives the handoff. Use the app's normal update channel if an update is available. Do not install an unofficial package or browser extension simply to repair a product link.

If the service offers an ordinary open-in-browser or open-in-app choice, try the appropriate supported option once and record the result. A link that reaches the app home screen has not successfully opened the product. Search for the exact product identifier or title only if that search is available in the current interface. Treat a search result as a new candidate until its image, options and listing identity have been checked against the original.
## Confirm your real region and delivery destination
A shared link can behave differently for people in different markets. Check the country or region associated with the current account and the actual delivery destination requested by the service. A product being visible to another user does not prove that it is available for your address. Equally, an unavailable item does not automatically mean the original creator posted a fake link.

Use your real destination. Changing settings to misrepresent where you live is not a reliable solution, because it cannot establish delivery eligibility, payment acceptance or after-sales coverage. If the service does not support an item or destination, stop the purchase attempt and look for a genuinely available alternative. Language and delivery country are also separate settings: viewing a Spanish page does not confirm delivery to every Spanish-speaking country or territory. Check the actual address, including islands and remote areas.
## Recheck the item, not just the HTTP result
A page can return normally while showing the wrong product. Compare product type, title, first image, visible seller or listing identifier and the option you intended to inspect. A database migration can change product numbers between sites. Replacing an old domain while keeping the same numeric path may therefore open a completely unrelated item. A shoe page is not a valid replacement for a hoodie page merely because both addresses load.

When maintaining a spreadsheet, verify the destination after every URL change. Keep the original picture only when it still represents that exact listing. If you cannot establish a match, mark the row for rechecking instead of presenting an unrelated product as a repaired link. This identity check matters more than a green availability indicator: it protects the reader from acting on a comparison between two different products.
## Separate an unavailable variant from a removed listing
A working listing may no longer offer the colour, size, material or bundle shown in a saved card. Open the available selectors and check the exact option. A low starting price can belong to a different variant. A gallery may show several colours even when only one remains selectable. Save the option name and current image together so that your shortlist does not silently switch products during the repair.

If the entire listing has been removed, a similarly named result is not automatically its continuation. Start a fresh evidence check for the new result, including measurements, contents and terms. Do not copy old reviews, prices or quality assumptions into the replacement row. When a high-consequence detail is missing, choosing no replacement yet is a better result than making the spreadsheet look complete with an unsupported match.
## Isolate the failure with one controlled comparison
Use a single known-working destination from the same service as a comparison. If it opens normally while the original fails, the evidence points toward a link-specific or listing-specific problem. If both fail, check the current service notice, account state and ordinary connection before editing a whole spreadsheet. This is a diagnostic clue, not proof of a particular cause. Avoid mass-changing links based on one failed click.

Change one thing at a time and record what changed: copied address, supported opening method, account selection or available variant. Repeatedly switching several settings makes the successful step impossible to identify. Do not clear important account data or saved records as the first remedy. If a sign-in is genuinely required, use the service's established sign-in flow rather than entering credentials into a page whose identity is uncertain.
## Finish with a verified destination or a clear unresolved note
A repaired link should pass four checks: it reaches the intended service, shows the intended product, preserves the exact option needed for comparison, and remains usable for your actual destination. Record the date and the evidence that changed. Then reopen the link from the card or article itself, because a correct URL in an editing file does not prove the published button uses it.

If the problem remains, report the source page, the affected link and the visible result. Explain whether the failure concerns opening, identity, region or variant availability. Exclude private tokens and account details from public reports. For purchase or account problems, use the service responsible for the order; an independent spreadsheet cannot recover an account or guarantee stock. The goal is a dependable next step for the reader, not a claim that every link will work permanently in every country.

## Keep a repair record that another reader can follow
For a hypothetical broken card, record the old destination, the observed failure, the proposed replacement and the evidence that connects the replacement to the same item. Add the exact selected variant and the date of verification. If the old card shows a backpack while the proposed page shows shoes, the replacement fails even when its server responds normally. If the title matches but the first image or included bundle differs, leave the repair pending until the identity is clear. After publishing a correction, open the visible card and its language versions to confirm that each uses the verified destination. Do not mark the whole catalogue checked after testing only one card. This small record makes future maintenance easier because the next editor can understand the reason for the change instead of repeating an unsupported domain substitution.
