ACBuy Link Not Working? Find the Original Taobao or Weidian URL

When an ACBuy spreadsheet link stops opening, the most useful thing to recover is the seller’s product page. It lets you check whether the item still exists, whether your size is available and whether the buying platform can read the listing. Keep the original spreadsheet row open while you work. A link to the correct seller and a record of the exact option you wanted are worth saving separately.

Research note

Use the sources below alongside the information shown for your own item, order or parcel.

01

Start with the button you actually clicked

Suppose a spreadsheet row shows a navy jacket in two fits. Clicking the photo opens a larger image, while the button beneath the price opens a product page. The image can help you recognise the jacket, but it cannot tell you which seller listed it or which fit was selected. Look for a separate source link, shop name or link attached to the product title. On a computer, copy that link’s address. On a phone, use the link menu or the product’s share button rather than the browser’s button for sharing the spreadsheet itself.

Paste the address into a private note first. This makes it easier to see if you copied a product, the whole spreadsheet, a search results page or a shop homepage. If the item opens in the marketplace app, navigate to its individual product screen before copying. Where the buying platform provides a link back to the seller, use it and check the destination. Keep both addresses until you have identified the product.

02

Read the marketplace and its listing number together

On a full Taobao product page, look for item.taobao.com/item.htm?id=NUMBER. On Tmall the common form is detail.tmall.com/item.htm?id=NUMBER. Weidian often uses weidian.com/item.html?itemID=NUMBER. For 1688, look for detail.1688.com/offer/NUMBER.html. NUMBER represents the digits you actually see. These examples describe the layout of an address. They do not refer to items for sale. Mobile and app share links can look different.

On Taobao or Weidian, removing everything after the question mark would remove the listing number. On 1688, the offer number sits between /offer/ and .html. A nearby SKU number may identify a particular choice within a listing. A shop ID identifies a seller, and an order or tracking number refers to a transaction or parcel. Only combine a marketplace and number that appeared together on the source page. A familiar photo appearing on another marketplace is too little evidence to match the listing.

03

Recover a link hidden inside another link

A buying-agent address may carry the seller URL inside a parameter such as url or productUrl. For example, the text https%3A%2F%2Fweidian.com is an encoded way of writing the start of a Weidian address. If the product page offers a source link, copying it directly is usually simpler than editing the wrapper. Otherwise, read the embedded address in your own browser or use a local URL parser. Preserve the seller’s parameter containing the item number.

Check the destination’s actual hostname before opening it. A marketplace name can appear in the path or query of a different website. If all you can see is an internal product number with no identifiable marketplace, keep the original link and request the source from the person who shared it. Sending the public item address is sufficient for identification. Keep account pages, checkout links and private order references out of shared spreadsheets.

04

When a short link opens the app, a login page or a homepage

An app share may include an invitation, a title and a short URL in the same message. Keep a copy of the whole message. Follow a recognisable link from a trusted source, then look at the destination after the app or browser finishes opening. A store banner is one step early. Open the item that has its own product name, price and choices, then share that screen. If the message opens the app store or homepage, ask the sender for a fresh share directly from the product.

If a marketplace asks you to log in or says the item cannot be viewed in your region, the product’s availability is still uncertain. Use your usual official app or ask the seller for a public listing. Do not give a third-party link tool your credentials or share a screen containing account details. A short code alone does not provide a reliable way to reconstruct an item number.

05

Match the variant before saving a replacement

Return to the jacket example. The listing title and hero photo match, yet the default option is black in a regular fit. Check the seller name, original colour wording, fit, size and any version or batch label. A translated option such as “blue” can hide a distinction between two shades. Copy the seller’s wording beside your own note. If a size chart is shown, record the measurement relevant to your choice and whether the chart describes the garment laid flat or the wearer.

Reopen the saved link in a fresh tab and see which options remain selected. A product URL can lead to the right listing while losing every selection you made. Write the quantity and selected-option price alongside the link. With a 1688 listing, also check the minimum quantity and which price tier applies to your quantity. A price attached to the smallest accessory or the largest quantity can look attractive in a spreadsheet and change when you choose the actual item.

06

Separate a missing listing from an import problem

If the seller page opens and shows your exact option, but ACBuy cannot display the item, try pasting that public seller address into the platform’s product search. ACBuy’s shopping guide describes importing a marketplace URL and a manual order flow for items that require assistance. Check which options are currently offered in your own account. A manual form is a request for assistance and does not confirm stock, price or the ability to place a new order.

If the listing opens but your size is greyed out or unavailable, record the link as valid and the variant as unavailable. If the seller explicitly says the listing was removed, ask whether it was relisted. A new listing number deserves a fresh check of the seller, description, measurements, quantity and price. If the only result is a login prompt or a technical error, record what you saw and leave availability open. These distinctions make the next check much quicker.

07

Use warehouse photos and support with the right reference

A warehouse QC photo can help compare buttons, stitching, measurements or labels when its listing and variant match. A photograph from an older order cannot tell you that the seller still has the same version today. Keep the original seller URL beside the image reference. If you found a possible replacement through image search, treat it as a new candidate and repeat the seller and option checks before attaching the photos.

For an existing order, use its private support conversation. Include the public source URL, the seller name, the variant as written on the listing and the screen where the link failed. State what you need the agent to confirm, such as whether the seller moved that exact navy jacket to another listing. Share the order reference only through the account’s official support channel. A public screenshot can usually be cropped to show the product title, options and error alone.

08

What belongs in the spreadsheet after the check?

Save one row per option when differences matter. For the jacket, that might read “seller name / Taobao item number / navy, relaxed fit / size L / selected price / last checked date”. Keep the seller URL and ACBuy link in separate columns. If a replacement is confirmed, preserve a short note identifying the old listing and what changed. This gives the next reader a way to verify the find even when one of the links expires again.
